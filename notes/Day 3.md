## 🇯🇵 Japan — Top Startups

### Top 10 Overview
1. **Mujin** — Robotics ($36.2B JPY raised, #1 in 2025)
2. **Mercari** — E-commerce Marketplace ($1.3B total funding)
3. **Tier IV** — Autonomous Vehicle Software ($1.25B funding)
4. **GITAI** — Space Robotics
5. **Olive Union** — Smart Hearing Aids
6. **Sagri** — AgTech/Agriculture Software
7. **Cogent Labs** — AI/ML Engineering ($58M)
8. **Parallel Inc.** — Social Network Gaming
9. **Virtusize** — Fashion Tech (Virtual Fitting)
10. **FiNC** — Health/Wellness Analytics

### My 3 Favourites

#### 1. **Mujin** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Why:** Industrial robotics with a software-first approach. Leader in 2025.
- **Impact:** #1 funded startup in Japan (JPY 36.2B), solving real manufacturing problems
- **Business Model:** Robotics-as-a-service, cloud-based control
- **Lesson:** Japan's strength is hardware + software blend. Mujin nails that.

#### 2. **Mercari** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Why:** Japan's marketplace giant. 2,000+ team, $1.3B funding, proven model
- **Impact:** Regional dominance, expanding globally
- **Business Model:** C2C marketplace (like eBay/Poshmark)
- **Lesson:** You can scale massively in Asia without needing Silicon Valley validation

#### 3. **Tier IV** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Why:** Self-driving car stack. Critical infrastructure for future mobility.
- **Impact:** $1.25B funding, open-source approach (Autoware)
- **Business Model:** Software licensing + partnerships with automotive OEMs
- **Lesson:** Open-source + enterprise can coexist beautifully. Community + money.

- **Impact:** Massive influence, billions of users, AI-driven recommendation engine
- **Business Model:** Ads + Content + Emerging AI products

- **Impact:** Disrupting the proprietary LLM narrative, very efficient training
- **Business Model:** Open-source + potential API/services
- **Lesson:** You don't need the biggest budget to be competitive. Efficiency matters.

#### 3. **Alibaba** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Why:** Qwen models are the most-downloaded LLMs on Hugging Face (2025-2026).
- **Impact:** Shifted the conversation: open-source from China is now competitive
- **Business Model:** Cloud services + AI models + e-commerce synergy
- **Lesson:** Diversified companies can leverage multiple business lines to win in AI

## Personal Reflections

1. **USA trend:** The race is vertical AI + safety. Every new AI startup is trying to own a vertical (medical, legal, etc.) or solve alignment.

2. **Japan trend:** Robotics + hardware is not dead—it's evolving. Mujin's cloud-first approach is the future of manufacturing.

3. **China trend:** Open-source AI from China is legitimately competitive. The narrative of "proprietary Western models dominate" is shifting.

4. **For my e-commerce app:** I could learn from:
   - **Mercari's** marketplace model (community-driven)
   - **ByteDance's** recommendation algorithm thinking
   - **Alibaba's** multi-service approach (sell platform + infrastructure)
